## The deep-lane verdict

**ALIVE**, on real shards, full configured scale: val loss **3.2603 → 3.1326**, top-1 **0.1839**
against a straw-man baseline of **0.0132** (14×), sampling **1000/1000 legal**, 20 s CPU. The
pre-registered criterion (`LOG/session-0007-preregistration.md`, committed before the run) is met
on both conditions.

One honest note carried into the LOG: the config's initial learning rate made the model diverge
(val 3.43 → 14.72, DEAD). A recorded sweep (1.0 / 0.2 / 0.05 / 0.01) showed only the high rates
diverge; the config now carries `lr: 0.05`. A hyperparameter fix, not a criterion change — recorded
because a silent tweak after seeing the numbers is the retouching ADR-0011 §5 forbids.
