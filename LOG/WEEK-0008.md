## The transport thread (the week's real risk)

Three incidents, one shape — a model typed the bytes and they arrived wrong:

- **0005** — an incomplete tree was published.
- **0008** — a file landed as mojibake (blob `d34a2329` ≠ local `af336cd0`).
- **0011** — two base64 characters lost (blob `bfea76b5` ≠ local `7e0e7530`).

Each was caught only by comparing the returned blob sha against the local `git hash-object`.
**ADR-0012** now bounds the risk: inline base64 only under 2 KB, mechanical transport above it, and
a verify duty on every file. Session-0012 proved the mechanical path byte-exact (152/152 blobs) and
published the whole session-0011 tree that had never reached the repository.

## The science thread

- The v2 family **does not beat v1** at this scale: every delta is negative and significant
  (d256_k8 −0.0356, d128_k8 −0.0366, d256_k0 −0.0453, d128_k0 −0.0435).
- The v2 family **diverges** at both tested learning rates (val loss 3.35 → 25.2, grad norm
  4.8 → 648). The open question is stability, not capacity.
- The **plateau detector cannot tell a plateau from a divergence** — it reported `PLATEAUED` on a
  run that blew up.
- The **verdict rule had a polarity defect**: `favours == "b"` (a v1 win) was read as a v2 win.
  Fixed, with a mutation control.
